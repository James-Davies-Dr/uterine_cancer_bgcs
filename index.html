Where to Operate (BGCS p.56)

Presumed FIGO Stage IA, Grade 1 or 2 endometrioid carcinoma with normal nodes may undergo surgery by a gynaecologist at a Diagnostic Cancer Unit who is a core member of an MDT (Grade D).

Lymph Node Management (BGCS p.59) Grade A
No Lymphadenectomy Recommended
Lymphadenectomy of non-bulky nodes is not recommended in low-risk EC (Stage IA G1/G2; <2cm nodal risk is ~1.4%) as it does not improve survival and significantly increases lymphoedema morbidity (Grade A).
Pre-Op Staging Imaging (BGCS p.57) Grade D

Pelvic Ultrasound / MRI Pelvis (to assess myometrial depth) + Plain Chest X-Ray (CXR) alone. Routine pre-op CT of the pelvis/abdomen in low-grade Stage IA disease has very low yield and is NOT indicated (Grade D).

Hysterectomy Route & Extent (BGCS p.58–60) Grade A/B

Total Hysterectomy + Bilateral Salpingo-Oophorectomy (BSO) without vaginal cuff or parametrectomy via Minimal Access Surgery (Laparoscopic or Robotic-assisted preferred over laparotomy, Grade A/B) within an Enhanced Recovery (ERAS) programme (Grade A).

Adjuvant Systemic Chemotherapy (Sec 8.2) Grade A

Adjuvant cytotoxic chemotherapy is not recommended for Low, Intermediate, or node-negative LVSI-negative Stage I–II endometrioid carcinoma outside clinical trials.

Uterine Leiomyosarcoma (uLMS) — BGCS Section 12.1
Primary Surgery & Nodal Rules:

Extrafascial Total Hysterectomy +/- Bilateral Salpingectomy (Grade D). Oophorectomy in young pre-menopausal women is NOT mandatory (2% ovarian metastasis rate; no OS impact, Grade D). Routine pelvic LND is NOT recommended (Grade D). Avoid morcellation (3-fold recurrence increase).

Adjuvant Therapy & Contraindications:

Early Stage (I–II): Observation is standard of care (Grade B). Routine adjuvant RT (EORTC 55874) and adjuvant chemotherapy (GOG-277) do NOT improve survival (Grade B). Adjuvant RT only for selected high-risk cases (e.g., positive excision margins, Grade B). Stage III–IV R0: Adjuvant chemotherapy can be considered (Grade C).

Advanced / Recurrent Management:

First-line single-agent Doxorubicin (Grade B) OR Gemcitabine + Docetaxel (Grade C). Second-line: Trabectedin (ORR ~59.6%, Grade C). Low-volume indolent ER/PR+ uLMS: Consider aromatase inhibitors (letrozole/anastrozole, Grade D). Olaratumab or Bevacizumab combinations are NOT recommended (Grade B).
